## The problem
When someone leaves their phone unattended, they may want to review reported activity on selected apps. The design challenge is to make the monitoring experience understandable without implying certainty that the operating system cannot provide.

## My contribution
I independently designed and built the native SwiftUI application, spanning onboarding, monitoring experiences, evidence timelines, subscription flows and the supporting website.

## Interaction decisions
- A simple **choose → arm → check** journey gives the monitoring session a clear beginning and end.
- Activity receipts distinguish reported activity from assumptions about what happened.
- “No activity reported” communicates the evidence available without claiming that nothing happened.
- Explanations make Screen Time authorisation and reporting limitations part of the experience.

## Product boundaries
Reports can be delayed or incomplete, and brief activity may not be reported. Receipt times indicate report arrival rather than exact app-opening times. The app does not identify who used the phone or read app content.
